LumiCubes — Privacy Policy
Effective date: 28 October 2026
Operator: Land Of Game
Privacy and support: programing995@gmail.com

LumiCubes is a creative building game designed for children aged 3–12. There are no player accounts, chat, user uploads, in-app purchases or our own analytics servers.

Local game information
Worlds, buildings, settings, selected blocks and progress are stored in the app's private storage on the device. These files are not uploaded by our game. Deleting a world removes it from the active list and moves its files to a local recovery folder on the device. Recovery copies remain until app storage is cleared or the app is uninstalled. Uninstalling normally removes the app’s local data. Device backup services controlled by the device or operating system may retain backups. We cannot remotely retrieve or delete those backups.

Optional decoration ownership and active building time are also stored locally. Deleting an individual world does not erase this profile progress. Clearing app storage or uninstalling removes it. It is not uploaded to our servers.

Advertising and children
The Android app integrates Google AdMob: Google Mobile Ads Unity Plugin 11.6.0 and Google Mobile Ads Android SDK 25.5.0, with Google's User Messaging Platform. All users receive child-directed treatment, a G content rating request and no personalized advertising or remarketing. Publisher first-party IDs are disabled. The Android advertising ID and Android Ad Services permissions are removed from the app manifest. We do not ask children to enter names, dates of birth or contact details.

A single banner may appear in the main menu or pause menu, in a separate area below the controls. It is hidden during active play, on other screens, and when the screen has insufficient space. An interstitial ad may appear only when you return to the main menu after a break, never during active play or at launch. Fullscreen ads are limited to one per five minutes, including a five-minute delay after launch. Gameplay and game audio pause during the ad. Rewarded advertising is disabled. Playing and saving worlds works without an internet connection; advertising requires one.

Data handled by Google
Ad requests contact Google. Depending on SDK operation and restrictions, Google may process IP addresses (which can indicate approximate location), ad and app interactions, technical diagnostics and device or other identifiers for ad delivery, measurement and fraud prevention. Child-directed settings reduce advertising data use; they do not make advertising data-free. Our app does not request GPS location, microphone, camera, contacts or access to your photos. We do not provide Google with a player's name or a game world.

Google operates its advertising systems under its own privacy and retention practices: https://policies.google.com/privacy and https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads . SDK disclosures: https://developers.google.com/admob/unity/privacy/play-data-disclosure . Advertising communication is encrypted in transit. Local files remain subject to device security. No service can guarantee absolute security.

Consent and choices
The app uses Google's UMP with the under-age-of-consent flag. A child is never asked to consent as an adult. Advertising starts only when the SDK indicates requests are permitted. Privacy options are available when required by UMP. You can play offline to avoid network ad requests. There is no paid ad-removal offer.

Retention, parents and rights
We retain no game data on a developer server. Google controls retention of the data it receives under the policies linked above. Parents or guardians may contact programing995@gmail.com about privacy, applicable access or deletion rights, or accidental personal information. Please do not send a child's personal information unless needed to handle a request. Support email is used to answer the request; it is not used for advertising. Relevant correspondence may be retained as necessary to resolve the request and meet legal obligations. Google-related requests may require contacting Google.

Changes
We will update this policy when data practices change and revise the effective date. Contact Land Of Game at programing995@gmail.com with questions.
